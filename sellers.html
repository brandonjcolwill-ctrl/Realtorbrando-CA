The money side

What selling actually costs.

No surprises at the closing table. Here’s where the money goes.

  • Commission: Typically around 5% of the sale price in total, split between the listing and buying brokerages, plus HST. It’s always negotiable, and I’ll put the exact numbers in writing before we list.
  • Legal fees: Roughly $1,500 to $2,500 for a real estate lawyer to handle the transfer.
  • Prep costs: Minor repairs, staging, deep cleaning. We’ll decide together what’s worth spending and what isn’t.
  • Mortgage discharge: Your lender charges a discharge fee, and breaking a term early can mean prepayment penalties. Check your mortgage terms before you list.

Seller FAQ

Questions sellers ask me.

  • When is the best time to sell? Spring brings the most buyers, but serious buyers shop year-round. Pricing right matters more than the month.
  • What if my home doesn’t sell? Then the price or the presentation is off, and we fix it with fresh eyes: new photos, adjusted price, or a new launch angle. I’ll be straight with you about which.
  • Should I buy first or sell first? Selling first gives you a firm budget; buying first avoids rushed decisions. In a balanced market, many sellers do both at once with the right conditions.
  • Do I need to stage my home? Not always fully, but decluttering and a few targeted fixes almost always pay for themselves in photos and showings.
  • How long will it take? Well-priced GTA homes often see action in the first two weeks. I’ll give you an honest timeline based on your neighbourhood and price point.
