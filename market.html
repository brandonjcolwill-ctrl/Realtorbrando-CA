October 2026 — covering September

Sales hit an eight-month high as prices keep easing

  • 5,765 homes sold in September, seasonally adjusted — up 2% from August and the strongest month since January.
  • $971,500 MLS® Home Price Index composite benchmark — down 0.5% month-over-month, and down 5.5% from a year ago.
  • +8.5% sales vs. last September, with +4% new listings year-over-year.
  • 2.5% Bank of Canada benchmark rate after September’s cut — the first since March, and a three-year low.

What it means: buyers are getting more negotiating room as prices soften, while sellers who price accurately are still moving homes — sales are running well ahead of last year. If borrowing costs keep easing, expect more buyers to come off the sidelines.

Source: TRREB Market Watch, September 2026.
